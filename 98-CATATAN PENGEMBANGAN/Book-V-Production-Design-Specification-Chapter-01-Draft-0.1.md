# Book V — Production Design Specification — Chapter 01

**Status:** CATATAN PENGEMBANGAN / NON-CANON.  
**Basis:** Visual Storyboard Chapter 01 Draft 0.1; Chapter 01 manuscript Revision Draft 0.4; Character Bible; Location Bible; visual-language exploration; Scene 1–4 page-ratio tests.  
**Scope:** production guidance for the 27 panels in Chapter 1 Scenes 1–4.  
**Purpose:** consolidate tested visual constraints before any illustrative page is produced. This document does not approve final art direction, character designs, location designs, or publication specifications.

## 2. Visual hierarchy

Use this order of attention unless an individual panel requires a different emphasis:

1. The action or decision that advances the scene.
2. Story-relevant document or prop.
3. Character blocking and relationship between participants.
4. Environmental context needed to understand the action.
5. Background activity and decorative detail.

Do not add background detail merely to make a panel look full. Quiet space is acceptable, especially around documents and moments of reading.

## 3. Panel and camera guidance

- Preserve storyboard panel order and each panel's narrative function.
- Use wide shots to establish a place or show the flow of people; use medium shots for interactions; use inserts for documents and hand actions.
- Vary shot size to avoid repetitive sequences of identical medium group shots.
- Keep panel transitions legible. Do not crop out the action that explains why a character or document matters.
- Use top-down or over-shoulder views when they clarify document relationships, not as decorative repetition.
- Do not force every scene into the same panel grid. The page ratio is a candidate canvas, not a requirement that all pages have identical panel counts or geometry.
- The page-ratio boards are layout studies only. They do not define final page count, gutters, bleed, trim, or binding.

## 4. Character depiction constraints

The Character Bible establishes identity, role, motivation, strengths, weaknesses, and arcs for six main characters. It does not establish their final visual appearance.

Until designs are approved:
- Use blocking, cropped views, rear views, neutral silhouettes, and hands/props where these preserve the action.
- Do not assign definitive facial features, hairstyle, clothing, body proportions, accessories, colors, or age appearance based on placeholder art.
- Keep each character identifiable through position and action only where the storyboard supports it.
- Neral Voss is a Book V draft character and NON-CANON. Do not establish a definitive appearance, gender presentation, age, costume, or personal insignia through production placeholders.
- Background visitors and workers remain generic; do not give them named identities or implied histories.

Character design approval is a separate future decision.

## 5. Location depiction constraints

The Location Bible only supports the listed CANON names and facts. Orlenn, Balai Catatan Perang, the inn, rest stop, and other Book V settings are draft material and NON-CANON.

For Chapter 1:
- Northglass must remain consistent with the established Book IV context. Do not redesign the stone, invent access routes, or turn the road into a fortress/city.
- Orlenn's street plan, skyline, gate design, architecture, signage, building materials, and civic identity are not established.
- Balai Catatan Perang may be shown as broad and externally simple, with functional access zones required by the scene. Do not make it a palace, fortress, temple, or monumental fantasy archive.
- Reception, public-copy shelves, restricted door, reading room, and tables are only spatial requirements for the storyboard; exact floor plan and furniture design remain open.
- Do not add crests, flags, emblems, decorative scripts, or institutional uniforms.

Location design approval is separate from this specification.

## 6. Document and prop standards

Documents are central to Chapter 1 and must remain consistent across appearances.

- Keep the same map folder and recurring map/document sheets visually consistent within the chapter.
- **NVR AGAIN** may be legible exactly as written. Do not create a logo, icon, glyph, visual effect, or implied meaning for the phrase.
- All other document text should remain unreadable placeholder lines unless the manuscript explicitly supplies wording.
- Scene 3 catalogue privacy information must not reveal invented names, addresses, or personal details.
- Scene 4 may show only the labels supported by the manuscript: event date, report-recording date, copy date, and collection-receipt date (in the four-column comparison table).
- The LEAX catalogue entry is a separate document and may only convey that LEAX disappeared after the Great War. Do not depict LEAX, the disappearance, a location, or any reconstruction.
- Do not invent calendar names, date values, regional marks, source names, historical factions, or additional chronology.

## 7. Color, texture, lighting, and typography

These areas remain open for controlled tests:
- palette and accent-color strategy;
- line weight and edge treatment;
- texture frequency and paper treatment;
- lighting contrast and shadow softness;
- dialogue lettering, narration, captions, labels, and sound-effect typography;
- font families, sizes, weights, line spacing, and minimum readable text size.

Working constraints:
- Prioritize contrast and legibility over decorative effects.
- Avoid textures behind small text or dense tables.
- Keep document text and dialogue visually distinct.
- Do not use typography or color to imply an unapproved faction, magic system, institution, or historical symbolism.
- Do not imitate a named artist or specific copyrighted work as the art direction.

No font, palette, or print specification is selected by this draft.

## 8. Production acceptance checklist

A panel/page may proceed to review only when all applicable checks pass:

- [ ] Panel order and action match the storyboard.
- [ ] No event, character, prop, or historical claim has been added.
- [ ] Character appearance remains within the approved design status.
- [ ] Location details remain within the approved design status.
- [ ] Recurring props match their previous appearances.
- [ ] NVR AGAIN is written correctly and receives no invented symbol or meaning.
- [ ] LEAX is not depicted as a person or as part of a reconstructed event.
- [ ] No unsupported dates, calendar systems, names, addresses, emblems, or signage are legible.
- [ ] Document hierarchy is clear at intended viewing size.
- [ ] Panel composition makes the scene's action understandable.
- [ ] The asset is labelled NON-CANON until explicitly approved.

A failed check means revise or reject the asset; do not silently accept visual invention as lore.
