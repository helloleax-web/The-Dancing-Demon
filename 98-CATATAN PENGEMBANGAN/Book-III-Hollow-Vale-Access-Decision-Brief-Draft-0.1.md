# Book III — Hollow Vale Access Decision Brief — Draft 0.1

**Status:** CATATAN PENGEMBANGAN / NON-CANON.  
**Purpose:** present the unresolved creator decision that blocks final approval of Book III.  
**No option below is CANON until the creator chooses it.**

## The exact issue

The current Chapter 3 manuscript depicts Tavian, Seraphine, and other members entering portions of Hollow Vale that are already open and physically reachable. The group does not move objects, dismantle structures, or cross a known barrier. The manuscript also explicitly says they do not know who, if anyone, has authority over the site and that no formal permission has been established.

This keeps the physical inspection narrow, but it does not establish a legal or socially accepted basis for entry. The story must not quietly treat "there is no visible barrier" as permission.

## Available story paths

### Option A — Permission exists, but the scene has not shown it
- Establish that a relevant party with actual authority has granted limited permission for documentation.
- The authority, method of permission, scope, and limits must be specified by the creator; none are currently CANON.
- The manuscript and storyboard would need a small setup or explicit reference before entry.
- Benefit: the group can conduct the existing inspection without making unauthorized entry a central conflict.
- Cost: adds a new authority/permission detail that must be kept consistent later.

### Option B — They knowingly inspect without confirmed permission
- Keep the current entry and the explicit uncertainty.
- Treat the action as ethically and legally uncertain, not as approved or lawful.
- Do not invent a landowner, guard, authority, or permit.
- Benefit: preserves current scene structure and leaves room for consequences or later scrutiny.
- Cost: characters knowingly take a risk; the narrative must not frame the procedure as resolving the legal issue.

### Option C — They do not enter until permission is established
- Revise Chapter 3 so the group documents only from outside.
- The discovery of the interior storage room and index would need to be delayed or moved to a later scene after access is established.
- Benefit: avoids depicting entry without a basis.
- Cost: materially changes the discovery sequence and requires revisions to Chapters 3–6 and their storyboards.

## Current recommendation

Option B is the least disruptive to the existing draft, but it preserves the uncertainty rather than resolving it. It is only appropriate if the creator wants the group to take that risk and accepts that later consequences may follow. If the intended story does not include that kind of risk, choose A or C instead.

## Decision required

Choose A, B, or C. If A, the creator must also establish who or what grants the limited permission and its scope. Until this is decided, Book III remains **NOT FINAL**.

**CANON impact:** none until creator approval.
