# Book V — Tavian Panel Integration Test (Draft 0.1)

**Status:** NON-CANON / visual production exploration  
**Scope:** Compare three abstract body-proportion systems in a shared Chapter 1 panel composition.

## Purpose
Test whether the three provisional proportion directions remain readable within the same panel geometry and alongside a neutral secondary figure and map prop. This is a composition test, not a character-design decision.

## Candidates
- **P1 — Compact:** relatively larger head, compact torso and shorter limb impression.
- **P2 — Balanced:** intermediate head-to-body and limb proportions.
- **P3 — Elongated:** relatively smaller head and longer limb impression.

## Shared test conditions
- Same abstract panel area and composition for all candidates.
- Same neutral map prop and generic secondary figure.
- Same text/dialogue reserve zone.
- No face, hair, costume details, location-specific architecture, dialogue, or new story information.

## Observations
- P1 reads as the most compact; risk: may feel too short if panel scale changes.
- P2 distributes visual mass more evenly; risk: may remain generic without later supported distinguishing choices.
- P3 reads as the most elongated; risk: may consume more vertical panel space.
- The map remains visually separated from the torso in this schematic test.

## Limitations
This test does not validate anatomy, perspective, clothing, facial design, final rendering, or the final 4:5 panel layout. It does not establish Tavian's height or official proportions.

## Conclusion
No candidate is selected. The three systems remain NON-CANON. The next useful step is to compare panel-scale readability and character/prop separation against the actual Chapter 1 storyboard panel requirements, without adding unsupported scene details.
