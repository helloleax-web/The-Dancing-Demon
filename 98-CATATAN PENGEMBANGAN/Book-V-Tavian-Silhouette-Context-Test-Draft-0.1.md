# Tavian Rell — Silhouette Context Test — Draft 0.1

**Status:** CATATAN PENGEMBANGAN / NON-CANON.  
**Scope:** comparative test of three silhouette directions in three recurring actions.  
**Basis:** Tavian Character Arc and Character Bible; previous silhouette exploration Draft 0.1.  
**Purpose:** compare visual readability in context without deciding Tavian's appearance.

## 1. Test setup

Three placeholder directions were placed in the same three actions:
1. walking while carrying a map;
2. reading a document;
3. sitting and taking notes.

The actions are chosen as production tests for a survey assistant and do not add new story events. The figures are abstract composition placeholders, not character illustrations.

## 2. Candidate directions

| Direction | Silhouette characteristic | Initial observation |
|---|---|---|
| A — Field Jacket | Compact and light | Reads as the least bulky; potentially gives more freedom to arm movement |
| B — Layered Overshirt | More structured torso | Has a clearer layered torso mass; may read well in medium shots |
| C — Travel Workwear | Looser, more voluminous clothing | Stronger clothing volume; may become visually heavy in seated or close framing |

These are observations about the placeholder shapes only. They do not establish clothing, material, equipment, or costume facts.

## 3. Context observations

### Walking with a map
- A has the lightest outer silhouette.
- B retains a clear torso structure.
- C has the largest clothing mass and may need careful proportion control.

### Reading a document
- The document remains the primary focal prop in all three tests.
- The placeholder arms are not sufficiently detailed to assess hand anatomy, grip, or final pose.
- No option has a validated advantage yet.

### Sitting and taking notes
- All three can be staged at a table in abstract form.
- The test does not establish body proportions, seated anatomy, chair design, room design, or furniture.
- A future illustration test must check that sleeves, bag straps, and clothing volume do not obscure the writing action.

## 4. What the test establishes — and does not establish

It establishes only that the three abstract silhouette directions can be compared across the same three action categories.

It does **not** establish:
- Tavian's height, build, body proportions, skin tone, face, hair, or eye color;
- his clothing, footwear, bag, accessories, or exact map equipment;
- his posture, facial expressions, personality, or emotional range;
- a final rendering style, color palette, or costume;
- a preferred or approved design.

## 5. Current evaluation

No winning direction is selected. A is visually lightest, B most structured, and C most voluminous in this placeholder test. This is insufficient evidence to choose a final design.

Next useful test: make a controlled neutral pose sheet for the shortlisted silhouette(s) only after the creator selects which direction(s) to continue exploring. Until then, keep all three open.
