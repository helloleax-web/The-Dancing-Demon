# Book V — Tavian Character Blocking Integration — Draft 0.1

**Status:** CATATAN PENGEMBANGAN / NON-CANON.  
**Basis:** Visual Storyboard Book V Chapter 1, Scenes 1–4 (27 panels); Pose Mechanics Review; existing Tavian visual exploration.  
**Purpose:** place a neutral, undesigned Tavian mannequin into selected existing storyboard panels to check action hierarchy, prop scale, and composition.  
**Canon impact:** none.

## 1. Source constraints

- The storyboard is the controlling source for panel actions and composition.
- Tavian's face, hair, clothing, height, body proportions, skin tone, dominant hand, and accessories remain UNKNOWN.
- Orlenn and Balai Catatan Perang remain NON-CANON proposed settings.
- No new dialogue, historical fact, document content, location feature, or character ability is introduced.
- Mira's practical route knowledge remains her own contribution; Tavian must not visually take over her action.
- Scene 4's chronology table remains a working comparison, not a source of invented dates or calendar names.

## 2. Selected panel blocking checks

### Panel 1.2 — Map insert
**Storyboard basis:** close-up of Tavian's hands keeping the map from folding; the map contains the stone-location map copy, surface-condition notes, and the NVR AGAIN sheet.

**Blocking test:** crop to hands, map, and paper edges. Keep the text NVR AGAIN readable if included; all other document writing remains abstract marks.

**Review:** the action can be communicated without face, costume, or body design. Keep hand placement subordinate to the map's legibility.

### Panel 2.3 — Route walk
**Storyboard basis:** Mira leads the group through a narrowing road using wheel marks, stone arrangement, and small route markers; Tavian takes notes.

**Blocking test:** Mira's route-reading action occupies the primary visual emphasis. Tavian remains a secondary figure with a note-taking gesture.

**Review:** avoid staging Tavian as the guide, map expert, or person identifying the route. Do not invent the route marker's shape or meaning.

### Panel 3.8 — Reading table
**Storyboard basis:** Tavian chooses a table near a window and arranges work papers so each document has its own space.

**Blocking test:** use a medium or top-down composition. Separate paper groups through spacing and overlap only; do not add readable text or decorative archival objects.

**Review:** the action is about organizing work surfaces, not revealing a new document or making a discovery.

### Panel 4.5 — Chronology table
**Storyboard basis:** Tavian arranges the table into four columns: event date, recording date, copying date, and collection-receipt date; Kael checks the source of a submitted copy.

**Blocking test:** show four clearly separated columns and Tavian's hands arranging the sheet. Keep Kael's checking action distinct if both fit the panel.

**Review:** do not invent calendar names, date values, regional marks, or a fifth category. Keep the chronology table separate from the LEAX catalogue entry.

## 3. Cross-panel continuity

- Tavian's map remains the same working prop when it reappears; do not change its format or add markings without textual support.
- The note-taking action in Panel 2.3 must not imply a new field notebook design or a specific writing instrument.
- The papers at Panels 3.8 and 4.5 are work documents; their visual organization may change only as described by the scene.
- Keep character scale and silhouette neutral across all four panels. The mannequin is a blocking aid, not a body-design proposal.

## 4. Findings

- Panels 1.2 and 4.5 benefit from hand/document-focused framing; full-body character design is unnecessary.
- Panel 2.3 requires clear action hierarchy so Mira remains the route-knowledge focus and Tavian's note-taking stays secondary.
- Panel 3.8 needs negative space between paper groups to communicate organization without inventing document content.
- These are composition findings only. They do not validate a final pose, anatomy, costume, or proportion system.
