# Tavian Rell — Hand & Prop Interaction Study Draft 0.1

**Status:** NON-CANON / exploratory production material  
**Scope:** Schematic interaction study for map, documents, and writing tool.  
**Purpose:** Check whether basic prop gestures can be communicated clearly without committing to Tavian's physical design.

## Canon boundary

CANON supports Tavian Rell as a 21-year-old human survey assistant and establishes his motivations, strengths, weaknesses, and character arc. It does not establish his appearance, hand proportions, clothing, preferred tools, or personal habits.

This study therefore does not define:
- hand shape, size, age markers, or anatomy;
- a specific map format, document type, writing instrument, or archive furniture;
- clothing, accessories, colors, or material choices;
- a habitual gesture or personality trait inferred from grip.

All diagrams are NON-CANON placeholders.

## Study groups

### Map
1. Open map with two-point support.
2. Folded map carried in one hand.
3. Finger indicating a map edge or location.
4. Map held flat against a surface.

### Documents
1. Single sheet held by a corner.
2. Small stack supported at the side.
3. Document held with both hands while reading.
4. Page turned using an edge pinch.

### Writing tool
1. Writing grip.
2. Resting grip while holding the tool.
3. Tool placed beside notes.
4. One hand stabilizes the page while the other holds the tool.

## Preliminary observation

At schematic scale, all three prop families can be distinguished through simple hand-object relationships. The diagrams do not yet validate finger articulation, grip pressure, joint mechanics, perspective, or realistic contact shadows.

## Next validation pass

- Test finger articulation and thumb opposition in close-up.
- Check whether the grip remains readable at intended panel size.
- Validate contact points and object weight direction.
- Test left/right hand ambiguity without assuming handedness.
- Only after those tests, connect the gestures to a selected character silhouette.

## Decision

No character design, prop specification, or canon fact is approved by this study.
