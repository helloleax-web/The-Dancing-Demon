- The 13 Prasasti remain a set of in-world interpretive manuscripts written by a descendant of LEAX from Avarra.
- Prasasti I is titled **NVR AGAIN**.
- Its sound copy is the same copy kept together with the interpretive manuscript.
- The interpreter's perspective is not treated as objective omniscience.
- The name is revealed through discovery of the first Prasasti, not through a direct transliteration of the Northglass markings.
