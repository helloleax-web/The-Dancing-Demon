# Tavian Rell — Joint & Foreshortening Study Draft 0.1

**Status: NON-CANON / exploratory production note**  
**Scope:** abstract articulation landmarks, limb overlap, near/far scale, and foreshortening principles.

## Purpose
Continue the visual-production exploration without establishing Tavian's appearance. This study follows the volume-construction test and remains a schematic exercise, not a finished anatomy model.

## Studies
1. **Arm reach:** shoulder, elbow, and wrist alignment while reaching toward a map.
2. **Leg bend:** hip, knee, and ankle alignment; planted-foot and support-path readability.
3. **Foreshortening:** near and far limb scale, overlap, and partial occlusion.

## Observations
- Joint landmarks provide useful directional anchors for motion.
- Bending changes segment direction, not the underlying segment length.
- Near/far limbs require scale and overlap changes to communicate depth.
- A hand or prop should remain attached to a readable wrist/forearm direction.

## Limitations
The current board is schematic. It does not validate human anatomy, muscle structure, realistic joint limits, accurate perspective, or final rendering style. No character-specific anatomy has been established.

## Canon boundary
- Tavian's identity and characterization remain as already recorded in CANON.
- His physical appearance and proportions remain UNKNOWN.
- P1/P2/P3 proportion systems remain NON-CANON exploratory candidates.
- This study adds no facts to the story and does not modify Character Bible or any CANON file.

## Next production step
Run a pose mechanics review using a consistent neutral mannequin: reach, bend, seated posture, and carrying a map. Only after those mechanics are coherent should more detailed character design be considered.
