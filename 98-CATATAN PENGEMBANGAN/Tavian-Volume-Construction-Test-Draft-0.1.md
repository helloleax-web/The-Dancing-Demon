# Tavian Rell — Volume Construction Test Draft 0.1

**Status: NON-CANON.** This is a visual-development study only. It does not establish Tavian's body type, height, anatomy, clothing, or final design.

## Purpose
Test whether the three existing proportion directions (P1 Compact, P2 Balanced, P3 Elongated) can be represented as simple three-dimensional masses and compared across views.

## Scope
- Head, torso, pelvis, and limb masses represented with primitive shapes.
- Front, side, and three-quarter view comparisons.
- A preliminary check of body volume as a foundation for later pose and prop studies.

## Observations
- The three candidates remain visually distinguishable by their relative head, torso, and limb masses.
- P1 reads as the most compact mass arrangement.
- P2 remains an intermediate comparison baseline, not a selected design.
- P3 reads as the most vertically extended arrangement.

## Limitations
This sheet is not a valid anatomy or perspective validation. It does not test accurate joint placement, musculature, foreshortening, weight distribution, clothing behavior, or a fully constructed rotating 3D model. The primitives are schematic placeholders.

## Decisions
- No proportion system is selected.
- No physical trait is added to CANON.
- Character Bible and Tavian's character arc remain unchanged.
- All studies remain NON-CANON pending explicit approval.

## Next step
Run a focused joint-and-foreshortening study before claiming pose consistency: shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, and ankle landmarks; limb overlap; and near/far limb scale under simple perspective.
